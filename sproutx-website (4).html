The cycle

From rainfall reading to field action, in four steps

Every SproutX unit runs the same loop, continuously, across every registered field — so no storm or outbreak passes through unnoticed.

01 / SCAN

Reads the field

Onboard and satellite sensors track precipitation, humidity, soil moisture and leaf temperature across every registered plot, refreshed every few minutes.

02 / DIAGNOSE

Flags what matters

Conditions are matched against known risk patterns for potato blight and chili anthracnose, so early warnings reach the farmer before symptoms show.

03 / SPRAY

Rolls out and treats

The SproutX unit navigates to the affected rows and applies precise, calibrated fertilizer or treatment doses — no overspray, no guesswork.

04 / REPORT

Tells the farmer

A plain-language summary lands on the farmer's phone: what was found, what was done, and what to watch for next — in Sinhala, Tamil or English.

Meet the unit

SproutX — the robot that walks the rows for you

A solar-assisted ground unit built to handle uneven terrain across hill-country and dry-zone fields alike. It doesn't replace the farmer's judgment — it gets there first, so the farmer's time goes to decisions, not patrols.

🛰️

Precision navigation

GPS and terrain-mapped routing plans the shortest safe path between rows, even on hill-country slopes.

💧

Calibrated spraying

Dosage adjusts per plant and per zone, cutting fertilizer waste and runoff into nearby waterways.

📡

Always-on monitoring

Continuous soil and canopy readings feed straight back to the farmer's dashboard, day and night.

🔋

Solar-assisted runtime

Extended field time between charges, built for the long dry-zone stretches with limited grid access.
